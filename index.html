In one sentence

Shrink a cipher until every state fits in memory, and its cycles, its period and its weak keys stop being estimates and become facts.

At 4, 6 and 8 cards per deck, every state of the Daniels’ Cipher was counted: 6.5 billion states across 12 configurations, on one laptop.
